2. Commercial Internal Use

You may make production use of the Licensed Work for your organization's
internal purposes, provided that each distinct application using the Licensed
Work remains under both of the following limits:

(a) 500,000 aggregate live node and edge records; and

(b) three concurrently running GraphForge instances.

Your organization includes all entities under common control. Contractors
operating the Licensed Work solely on your organization's behalf are included
as part of your organization's use.

The limits apply collectively to all GraphForge projects and instances used
for the same application. You may operate separate installations for distinct
applications, but you may not divide, shard, or distribute one application
across multiple projects, installations, accounts, regions, or affiliates to
avoid either limit.

For purposes of the record limit, count the live node and edge rows in the
current committed generation of each GraphForge project used by the
application. Historical generations and knowledge, provenance, confidence,
evidence, and epistemic records are excluded.

A "running GraphForge instance" is an operating-system process or isolated
serverless invocation in which the Licensed Work is loaded and which has
opened a GraphForge project containing at least one live node. A container,
pod, virtual machine, or host is not counted separately from the processes or
serverless invocations running within it.

3. Prohibited Commercial Offerings

You may not use the Licensed Work in a commercial offering that:

(a) allows third parties to access GraphForge functionality by directly or
indirectly creating, supplying, managing, querying, searching, analyzing,
traversing, or retrieving graphs or graph-derived analytical results; or

(b) embeds or includes the Licensed Work in a closed-source product sold or
licensed to third parties.

Hosting or using the Licensed Work for your organization's internal purposes
is not a prohibited commercial offering.

A product does not become a prohibited commercial offering merely because
the Licensed Work is used as an internal implementation component, provided
that third parties are not offered graph database, graph management, graph
query, graph search, or graph analytics functionality.
